MENUS — FRONT DOOR & THE PAD
One scheme everywhere: ← → pick the panel (or the ship), ↑ ↓ walk its rows, A / ENTER act. Arrow keys, D-pad or left stick — and on glass, just tap the bar you want.
Parked at a port the panels stack into one column: OUTFITTER, DISPATCH, MARKET. One opens at a time; the closed bars still tell you what's inside.
TOUCH
Left thumb flight stick — touch anywhere on the left of the glass, the stick comes to you
Right thumb FIRE / THRUST / REVERSE / STOP · VIEW top-right
LANDING ASSIST pops up over a deck — tap it to set down, tap LAUNCH to leave
SALVAGE
Shoot junk for scrap, then fly through the wreck — it reels in on its own. Ease off the throttle to collect; a boosting ship outruns the beam. Ram anything and you spill cargo — swing back to scoop it up before it drifts off. Land anywhere to repair the hull.
Cargo in the hold can spill; the BANK can't. Banked scrap survives a wreck, and a reload.
CHARLESTON is home — full price for your hold, the OUTFITTER, the job board, and a sky nobody hunts you in. The gold HOME marker points the way. Other pads are just ports: each one pays what it pays and sells what it sells, and a pirate market pays less and protects you not at all. The landing tag tells you what you're standing in.
At the OUTFITTER — beam range, hull plating, cargo clamps. Click a row, press 1 / 2 / 3 / 4, or walk to it and press A / ENTER.
RAIDERS
A red strobe is a bandit — fight or run (you're faster). An amber blink is a neutral scavenger: it holds fire unless you shoot first. Fly close with guns cold and it hails you — E (or D-pad ↑) pays its fee from the bank and it flies your wing, two max. HOME's sky is a safe harbor — nobody hunts you there. A port's sky isn't.
